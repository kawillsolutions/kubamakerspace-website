More than a holiday activity

A place to explore, learn, and grow

Kuba Innovation Camp is a four-week holiday programme organised by Kuba Makerspace.

It introduces children and young people to practical skills, creativity, technology, entrepreneurship, and innovation. Participants learn by doing, experiment with ideas, work on projects, and develop confidence, teamwork, and problem-solving skills.

The camp aims to nurture the next generation of creators, inventors, entrepreneurs, and leaders.

Find a spark. Build a skill.
